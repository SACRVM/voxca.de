Voxcade 0.4.2: free, and open

Voxcade is free under the zlib licence, its source is open on GitHub, and this is the first version the launcher fetches by itself.

Two decisions, one small version.

Free, as LÖVE is

Voxcade is now under the zlib licence: use it for free, make games with it, give them away or sell them. A game you make with Voxcade is yours alone. The same goes for the five games that come with it and the templates: learn from them, build on them. LICENSE.txt and NOTICE.txt are in the package from this version on.

The source is open

The engine, the games, the IDE and the tools are on GitHub: github.com/SACRVM/voxcade. Pull requests are welcome; CONTRIBUTING.md there says what to check before sending one. Found a bug? An issue there reaches us, and a replay of the run (F9 in the game) helps most.

Getting it

Coming from 0.4.1, start Voxcade: the launcher says that 0.4.2 is there, and UPDATE NOW fetches it, checks it and puts it in place. Your games, settings and saves stay. New here? Download it and follow how to install.
